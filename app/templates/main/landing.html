From draft to released result

Three steps, the same for a five-question quiz or a full certification exam.

1

Author the test

Mix single-answer, multiple-answer, true/false, and written questions. Set a time limit and how long candidates have to start.

2

Dispatch to candidates

Upload a list or a CSV. Each candidate gets their own invite, their own clock, and their own randomized question order.

3

Grade and release

Objective questions score themselves. Written answers go to a review queue — graded blind, released to candidates on your schedule.

Grading stays fair by default

A few things ScoreRaven enforces on its own, not as an afterthought.

Blind grading

Graders see a candidate's answers, never their name.

Server-side timing

The clock runs on ScoreRaven's servers, not the candidate's browser.

One attempt, honestly enforced

A candidate can resume a dropped connection — never restart a finished attempt.
